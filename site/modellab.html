Weighting recall more heavily (Fβ)

Macro-F1 treats a false alarm and a missed warning as equally bad. For an early-warning system they are not — missing a high-risk country is the costlier mistake. Fβ makes that weighting explicit. We report F2, which counts recall twice as heavily as precision, because that matches how this system is meant to be used. (β = 1 is exactly the macro-F1 shown above.)

How much should a missed warning weigh?

Telling the model that the High tier matters more buys recall and costs precision. Rather than assume one setting, we swept five — no weighting, sklearn's automatic balanced, and High weighted 2×, 3× and 5× — and measured both sides of the trade. Scored on the validation split, never on test.
